# Heating base guide

This guide is for everyday use: installing your base, setting temperatures, getting alerts and
asking for help. Your base arrives assembled and tested: you only connect its heat mats, its
probes and its power supply.

## 1. What the base does

The base heats **two zones** of your terrarium, each with its own mat and probe, following the
temperatures you choose for day and night. It works **on its own, even without internet**: the
app lets you follow and adjust it from anywhere.

It also protects itself. If a probe stops answering, a zone gets too hot or a mat heats without
effect, it cuts the heating of that zone and tells you.

## 2. Installing a base

1. In the app, tap **+** and scan the label under the base (or type its reference).
2. Plug the base in. It creates its own Wi-Fi, "terrarium-config-…": join it with your phone and
   the base's page opens by itself. Choose your home Wi-Fi there and type its password.
3. Back in the app, each step is ticked as it happens. Give the base a name and choose the
   starting temperatures, or a schedule that follows sunrise and sunset.

That's it: the first chart appears, and the app offers to turn on alerts.

## 3. Every day

A base's home screen shows its two zones in large type: the temperature, whether the zone is
heating, and the next target. Tap a zone to change its temperature with the ‹ › arrows, in 0.5 °C
steps; the base receives it one second after your last tap.

The round buttons on the home screen:

- **Maintenance**: cuts the heating while you clean, then the base resumes by itself at the time
  shown.
- **Clear faults**: once the cause of a fault is fixed, restarts the heating of the zone.
- **Alerts** and **Updates**: the sections of the same name.

If the base stops answering, the app keeps its last temperatures, with the time they were read.
Meanwhile, it keeps heating on its own.

## 4. Scheduling the day

The **Schedule** section shows the current day on a 24-hour strip.

- **Day and night**: two temperatures per zone and the switching times. You can also follow
  sunrise and sunset.
- **Time slots**: up to four moments a day (morning, heat peak, evening, night), the same every
  day or a separate set for the weekend.
- **Follow the seasons**: in winter, temperatures drop a little and the day gets shorter, with a
  gradual transition.
- **Preheating**: the base learns to start early enough to reach the right temperature on time.
- **Holiday mode**: your departure and return dates. The base switches by itself and comes back
  to your usual settings by itself.

## 5. Charts and log

- **Charts**: temperatures over 6 hours, 24 hours, 7 and 30 days. Pinch to zoom, drag to move.
- **Log**: what happened, day by day (alerts, changed settings, day and night switches, power
  cuts).
- In the app settings, **Exports** prepares the monthly logbook as a PDF.

## 6. Getting alerts

- **Notifications**: in the **Alerts** section, turn notifications on. You are told about a
  fault, a temperature drifting away, a base that stops answering (after five minutes) and its
  return.
- **Alerts sent by the base**: also in **Alerts**, the app helps you receive the base's own
  alerts on your phone, app closed, with the free ntfy app.

## 7. Sharing a base

In the app settings, **Share a base** creates an invitation link: **read-only** (for someone
looking after your animals, for example) or allowed to change settings. You can remove an access
at any time.

## 8. When something goes wrong

- **A zone shows a fault**: the heating of that zone is cut, the other one keeps going. Advice is
  shown under the fault: check that the probe is plugged in and in its place, then tap **Clear
  faults**. If the fault comes back, ask for help.
- **The base stops answering**: check that it is powered and that your home Wi-Fi works. It keeps
  heating in the meantime.
- **You changed your router or Wi-Fi password**: after three minutes without Wi-Fi, the base
  reopens its "terrarium-config-…" network; join it and choose the new network, as during
  installation.
- **Asking for help**: the base's **Service** section. Describe what you see; the answer arrives
  in the same place, with a notification. If support asks you, open access to the base for 24
  hours: they can check and adjust it remotely, and you can close access at any time.

## 9. Updates

The base updates itself at night, when everything is fine. Heating keeps running during the
download and only stops for a few seconds while it restarts. The **Updates** section lets you
postpone an installation by a few days.

## 10. Your data

- **Lock code** (app settings): protects the app on a shared phone.
- What the app and the server keep, and for how long, is detailed on the **Privacy** page.
